School history

Ilado-Sagbo Community Grammar School, Iseyin

Established December 2004 by the Ilado and Sagbo communities; formally recognised by the Oyo State Ministry of Education, Science and Technology in January 2005.

150students at inception
2006moved to its permanent 15-acre site at Ere Stream
2007first BECE examination sat
2010pioneer set sat their SSCE final year exams
20BECE cohorts produced as of 2025/26
17SSCE/NECO cohorts produced as of 2025/26

Recognised by WAEC and NECO to conduct Senior School Certificate Examinations. BECE Centre Number: P16021.
